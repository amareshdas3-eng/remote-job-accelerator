## 3. Activation: Time-to-First-Useful-Application

The Primary Activation Milestone:
> **A user is activated the moment they select their first matched job and generate their first tailored resume/cover letter draft.**

```
Visitor (Landing Page)
   ↓ (Goal: 40% signup)
Signup & Resume Upload
   ↓ (Goal: 80% profile extraction)
Target Role & Evidence Ingestion
   ↓ (Goal: 90% first match view)
Select Job & Generate Tailored Pack
   ↓ (Goal: 75% reaching tailored pack)
✓ ACTIVATED USER (< 8 minutes total elapsed time)
```

**Optimization Levers**:
1. Zero empty states: Pre-seed 3 active remote engineering jobs immediately upon first login.
2. In-app onboarding guide (`OnboardingGuide.tsx`) with 4 clear steps and completion checklist.
3. 1-click sample resume upload for users testing the workspace without their file handy.
